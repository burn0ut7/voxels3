# Procedural rivers

direction: carve valleys into the base terrain and hold water at one shared

asked that its method be documented and preserved.

width/curve parameters, delta construction, resource bounds and limitations.

preserves the rejected straight, isolated-pool, short-course and elevated-water
iterations as well as the approved visual direction. Full runtime/performance
acceptance remains pending; visual approval does not substitute for those checks.
